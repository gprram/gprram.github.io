About

Learning is better when something moves.

I am G. Raghu Ram, a B.Tech graduate who trains learners in coding, robotics and artificial intelligence. My classes are built around a simple idea: every concept should end with something you can hold, run or watch drive across the table.

I work with school students, college teams and beginners from other fields. We start with sensors and motors, move into Python and logic, and finish with projects that use computer vision and machine learning.
